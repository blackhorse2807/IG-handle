    How it works

    What the person does after you send the message.

    1. Taps Step 1, then “Start chat”This opens a chat with your business account on Instagram. We reply asking them to tap Step 2.
    2. Taps Step 2The same chat opens again, and Instagram tells us which link they used.
    3. We match the link to the numberEach link belongs to one phone number. We save their @username and send “All set ✅”.

    Two links are needed because Instagram leaves out the link's code the first time someone messages a business. People who have messaged you before only need Step 2. Links only work on a phone, and expire after a while; use “New link” on an expired row to send a fresh one.
